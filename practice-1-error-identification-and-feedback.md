Practice 1: Error Identification and Constructive Feedback

User Prompt

Explain three benefits of drinking water in simple English.

AI-Generated Response

1. Drinking water helps prevent dehydration.
2. Water supports normal body functions.
3. Drinking exactly five litres of water every day is necessary for everyone to remain healthy.

1. Error Identification

Statement 3 contains the main error.

2. Error Analysis

The statement makes an unsupported universal claim by suggesting that everyone needs exactly five litres of water daily.

Individual water needs vary depending on factors such as physical activity, climate, diet, and health.

Presenting one fixed amount as necessary for everyone can mislead users.

3. Constructive Feedback

Remove the claim that everyone needs exactly five litres of water daily.

Explain that individual water requirements vary depending on activity level, climate, diet, and health. Avoid presenting one fixed amount as a universal requirement.

Key Lesson

AI evaluators should recognize unsupported universal claims and replace them with accurate, balanced explanations.

Final Verdict

The third statement is misleading because it presents a fixed water intake as a requirement for everyone.
