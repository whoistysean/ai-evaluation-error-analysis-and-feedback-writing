Error Analysis and Feedback Writing Guide

1. Purpose

Error analysis helps AI evaluators identify problems in generated responses and understand how those problems affect response quality.

Constructive feedback explains the problem and recommends a practical correction.

2. Common AI Response Errors

Unsupported Claims

Statements presented as facts without sufficient evidence.

Exaggerated Claims

Statements that overstate what something can achieve.

Absolute Claims

Words such as “always,” “never,” “everyone,” and “guarantees” can signal claims that require careful verification.

Misleading Health Information

Health statements that make unrealistic promises or ignore important individual differences.

3. My Three-Step Evaluation Method

Step 1: Identify the Error

Locate the specific sentence or statement that contains incorrect, misleading, or unsupported information.

Step 2: Explain Why It Is Problematic

Describe what makes the statement inaccurate, exaggerated, or misleading.

Step 3: Write Constructive Feedback

Tell the AI what to remove or correct and provide a more accurate alternative.

4. Qualities of Good Feedback

* Specific: Identifies the exact problem.
* Clear: Uses simple and understandable language.
* Accurate: Provides factually correct corrections.
* Constructive: Explains how the response can improve.
* Balanced: Avoids exaggeration and unsupported promises.

5. Important Lessons

Identifying an error is only the first step in evaluation.

A good evaluator must also understand why the error matters and communicate how it should be corrected.

Feedback should improve response quality without introducing new misinformation.

6. Conclusion

This guide demonstrates my beginner-level understanding of AI evaluation error analysis, factual reasoning, and constructive feedback writing.
