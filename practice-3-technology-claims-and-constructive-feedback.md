Practice 3: Technology Claims and Constructive Feedback

User Prompt

Explain three ways technology helps students learn. Use exactly three bullet points and simple English.

AI-Generated Response

* Technology gives students access to educational resources.
* Online learning platforms allow students to study from different locations.
* Technology guarantees that every student will achieve excellent grades.

1. Error Identification

The third bullet contains an unsupported guarantee.

2. Error Analysis

Technology can provide access to educational materials, online courses, and learning resources.

However, access to technology does not guarantee excellent grades for every student. Academic performance depends on several factors, including effort, teaching quality, learning conditions, and access to support.

3. Constructive Feedback

Remove the claim that technology guarantees excellent grades for every student.

Explain that technology provides access to educational materials, online courses, and learning resources that can support students’ understanding and academic progress. Avoid promising specific academic outcomes.

Key Lesson

AI evaluators should distinguish between realistic benefits and exaggerated promises. Constructive feedback should replace unsupported claims with accurate explanations.

Final Verdict

The response provides two valid benefits of technology but includes a misleading guarantee in its third bullet.
