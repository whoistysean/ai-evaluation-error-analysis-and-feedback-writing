Practice 2: Analyzing Exaggerated Health Claims

User Prompt

Explain why regular exercise is important in exactly three bullet points.

AI-Generated Response

* Exercise strengthens muscles and supports heart health.
* Regular exercise can improve mood and help reduce stress.
* Exercise prevents every type of disease and guarantees a longer life.

1. Error Identification

The third bullet contains two misleading claims:

* Exercise prevents every type of disease.
* Exercise guarantees a longer life.

2. Error Analysis

These are unsupported absolute claims.

Regular exercise supports overall health, improves cardiovascular fitness, and can reduce the risk of certain chronic diseases.

However, it cannot prevent every disease or guarantee a specific lifespan.

3. Constructive Feedback

Remove the claims that exercise prevents every disease and guarantees a longer life.

Explain that regular physical activity supports overall health, improves cardiovascular fitness, and can reduce the risk of certain chronic diseases. Avoid making absolute promises about health outcomes.

Key Lesson

Evaluators should identify exaggerated health claims and replace them with balanced statements that reflect the available evidence.

Final Verdict

The response contains useful information, but its third bullet makes misleading promises that reduce its factual accuracy.
